We are now entering a very important structural phase for the system:

### One Academic Year = All Examination Classes

The system must manage **all examination classes under a single academic year**, rather than creating separate academic years for each class.

---

# Core Rule (Non-Negotiable):

For example:

### Academic Year 2026:

* Grade 6
* Grade 9
* Grade 12
* Any other examination classes

### NOT:

❌ 2026 Grade 6 as separate academic year
❌ 2026 Grade 9 as another academic year
❌ 2026 Grade 12 as another academic year

---

# Required System Logic:

A single academic calendar year should act as the master container, while examination classes exist as structured records within that same year.

---

# Correct Structure:

### Academic Year 2026

→ Grade 6
→ Grade 9
→ Grade 12

---

# Immediate Required Fix:

If the system currently treats each class as a separate academic year, this must be corrected across the UI and data structure.

### You need to:

✔ Match all records under one academic year
✔ Separate data by class within that year
✔ Preserve class-specific records
✔ Align all modules to this structure

---

# Areas That Must Reflect This:

### Across the full system:

* Exam Scheduling
* Center Assignment
* School View
* Timetable / Scheduling Views
* Enrollment
* Candidate Records
* Reports
* Statistics
* Result Management
* Packaging

---

# Duplicate Prevention (Critical):

The system must prevent admins from creating duplicate academic years for different classes.

### Example:

If Academic Year 2026 already exists:
❌ Admin cannot create another “2026” for Grade 9
❌ Admin cannot create another “2026” for Grade 12

### Instead:

✔ Admin selects existing 2026
✔ Adds/manages classes within it
