The Center Timetable tab is currently incomplete because it is not properly displaying the published Exam Schedules from the Exam Scheduling system.

### Core Fix Required:

Each Examination Center must automatically display all relevant Exam Schedules directly from the Exam Scheduling page, with clear class-by-class timetable separation.

---

# Non-Negotiable Rule:

The Center Timetable tab should function as a live class timetable viewer for all examination classes assigned to that center.

---

# Required Center Timetable Logic:

If a center hosts:
✔ Grade 6
✔ Grade 9
✔ Grade 12

### Then the Center Timetable must display:

### Grade 6 Timetable

### Grade 9 Timetable

### Grade 12 Timetable

Each class must be clearly separated—not merged into one confusing schedule.

---

# Display Format Options:

### Preferred:

✔ Class Tabs
✔ Dropdown by Class
✔ Separate timetable cards
✔ Expandable schedule sections

---

# Example:

## Center ABC:

### Grade 6

* English
* Mathematics
* Science

### Grade 9

* Arabic
* Mathematics
* Social Studies

---

# School Dashboard Integration (Very Important):

Each school must also be able to view:

### 1. Their Assigned Examination Center

### 2. That Center’s Full Timetable

### 3. Only the timetable for examination classes the school actually has

---

# School Logic:

If School A has:
✔ Grade 6 only
→ Show only Grade 6 center timetable

If School B has:
✔ Grade 6 + Grade 9
→ Show Grade 6 and Grade 9 separately

---

# Prevent:

❌ Schools seeing irrelevant classes
❌ Mixed class timetable confusion
❌ Empty timetable pages when schedules exist

---

The system must connect:

### Exam Scheduling Page

→ Center Timetable Tab
→ School Dashboard

This should be a live synchronized structure, not manually duplicated schedules.
