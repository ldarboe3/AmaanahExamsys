A school that has both:

### Correct classification:

## BCS (Basic Cycle School)

# Updated Education Level Logic:

### Grade 6 only:

✔ LBS (Lower Basic School)

### Grade 9 only:

✔ UBS (Upper Basic School)

### Grade 12 only:

✔ SSS (Senior Secondary School)

---

# Combined Structure:

### Grade 6 + Grade 9:

✔ BCS (Basic Cycle School)

---

# Expanded Combined Structures:

### Grade 6 + Grade 9 + Grade 12:

✔ BCS
✔ SSS

(Or display as Basic Cycle + Senior Secondary depending UI design)

---

# Important Rule:

BCS replaces separate LBS display when both Lower and Upper Basic levels exist together.

### Example:

❌ LBS + UBS

### Replace with:

✔ BCS
