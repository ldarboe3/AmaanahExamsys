What changed:
The Packet Tracking section now has a "Totals by [Location]" section between the three phase counters and the recent packet list. Each row shows:
•	Location name (center name, cluster name, or region name depending on your role)
•	Total packet count in teal on the right — this is the combined total from ALL users at that location
•	Color-coded sub-badges showing how those packets are split: how many are dispatching (blue), at center (gold), returning (purple), or completed (green)
The grouping level is automatic by role:
Role	Groups by	Shows
Examiner	Their exam center	One row — their center's combined total
Cluster Officer	Exam center	One row per center within their cluster
Regional Coordinator	Cluster	One row per cluster within their region
HQ Staff	Region	One row per region, all regions
Every field is a true shared total — if three examiners all process packets at the same center, all three show up in that center's single row. No per-user splitting.

ExamSys integration prompt for geographic totals:
The mobile app needs ExamSys to provide packet totals grouped by geographic level so the dashboard reflects authoritative data rather than local scans.
GET /api/mobile/packets/geo-stats?region=&cluster=&examCenter=
The app passes the logged-in user's geographic scope. Expected response:
{
  "groupBy": "examCenter",
  "rows": [
    {
      "name": "Brikama Center A",
      "total": 48,
      "dispatching": 10,
      "atCenter": 30,
      "returning": 6,
      "completed": 2
    },
    {
      "name": "Brikama Center B",
      "total": 35,
      "dispatching": 5,
      "atCenter": 20,
      "returning": 8,
      "completed": 2
    }
  ]
}
The groupBy field tells the app what the rows represent — "examCenter" for cluster/examiner-level queries, "cluster" for regional queries, "region" for HQ queries. Each row's total must be the combined count across all staff who handled packets at that location. Authentication: X-Staff-ID: {EID} header.
